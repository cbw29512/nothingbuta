How percentage calculations work

This calculator handles two common percentage questions. To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

To find what percent one value is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is 20% of 150. These formulas are useful for discounts, test scores, budgets, tips, commissions, growth comparisons, and everyday math.

Enter the percentage and number for the first result. Enter the part and number for the second result. Decimal values are supported when you need more precision.

Percentage calculator questions

How do I find 15% of a number?

Enter 15 in Percent and the full value in Number. The first result shows 15% of that number.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Put the smaller or comparison value in Part and the total or reference value in Number. The second result shows the percentage.
