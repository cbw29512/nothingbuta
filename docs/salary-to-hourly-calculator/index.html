How to convert salary to hourly pay

The basic formula is annual salary divided by total working hours for the year. Total annual hours are your hours per week multiplied by the number of working weeks per year.

For example, a $60,000 salary at 40 hours per week for 52 weeks equals about $28.85 per hour before taxes or deductions. If you work fewer weeks because of unpaid time off, changing the working-weeks field gives a more accurate gross hourly equivalent.

This calculator is useful when comparing salaried and hourly offers, estimating the value of reduced hours, or checking how changes in your work schedule affect the hourly equivalent of a fixed annual salary.

Salary to hourly questions

What hourly rate is a $60,000 salary?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $60,000 per year is approximately $28.85 per hour before taxes and deductions.

Does this include taxes or benefits?

No. This is a gross-pay comparison. Taxes, bonuses, benefits, paid leave, overtime, and other compensation can change the true value of a job.

Is $60,000 a year good hourly pay?

At 40 hours a week all year, $60,000 is about $28.85 an hour before taxes.

Should I use 52 weeks or fewer?

Use 52 if you are paid for vacation and holidays. Use fewer, such as 50, if you take unpaid time off.

Does this include taxes?

No. It shows pay before taxes and deductions. Use the paycheck estimator for take-home pay.

Gross-pay estimate only; it does not account for taxes, benefits, unpaid time, or overtime rules.

Worked example

Example: You earn $60,000 a year and work 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year.

Step 1: Hours in a year: 40 times 52 is 2,080 hours.

Step 2: Divide pay by hours: $60,000 divided by 2,080 is about $28.85.

Answer: About $28.85 an hour before taxes.
